Learning curve: loss per cycle (RMS error of the predictions made during that drying period, before the gradient-descent update). It should fall over the first cycles, then level off.
Water plan error per cycle (how far the planned mL per % was from what the cycle really needed; 0 = perfect)

"Whole-period error" is the RMS error of every prediction made during that drying period, so it shows how good the controller is for most of the wait, not just its very first guess. "First predicted" is the time the controller first quoted after a watering cycle ended; "Actual" is how long it really took to reach the watering threshold. "Difference" is predicted minus actual: negative means the controller predicted too short a time, positive too long. Gain / offset are the two numbers the controller had learned (by gradient descent) when it made that prediction (×1.00 / 0 s = nothing yet). Drying periods interrupted by rain or manual watering are left out.
